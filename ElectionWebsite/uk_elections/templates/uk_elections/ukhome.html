    UK Election History

    Welcome to my site! As an election nerd and lover of stats and maps plus an amateur coder, I set out to merge all these interests in this site.

    The elections currently available are listed on the Elections page. From 1885 onwards geographical accurate election maps are available, before that only representative maps are available. For most elections hex maps are available showing each seat as a same-sized hexagon (note that some historic constituencies had multiple seats). For older elections only results are currently available but these will be expanded over time.

    The Constituencies page has a list of all constituencies, current and historic. All available election results are detailed on each constituency page together with a graph of party vote over time.

    Constituencies are allotted as accurately as possible to a county or counties which are listed on the Counties page. For each county is a graph showing the period of existance of each constituency.

    The People page lists some MPs and candidates and will be expanded over time. For elections before 1832, there are links to biographies on the History of Parliament website.

    The Parties page lists the major political parties and their results at each election.

    The Boundary Changes page shows the changes to constituencies over time.

    Finally, the Sources page lists the sources used in creating this site.

    It should be noted that "UK" includes (Southern) Irish constituencies from 1801 to 1921.
