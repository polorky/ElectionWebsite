Sources and References

This project has grown haphazardly over a number of years and I have chop and changed data sources and display methods over that time. This does regrettably mean that I can't precisely source everything I am using. However, below are all the sources that I bookmarked so, even if I didn't use them, they may well be useful to others.

  • Parliamentary Constituencies - Link - Provided geojson files for constituency boundaries back to 1885.
  • Parliament API - Link - Results going back to 1832. I originally pulled the results from Wikipedia but am checking them against this site. That said it has some disappointing errors/typos.
  • Wikipedia - Link - My main reference for results and constituency changes. I am trying to verify everything from more primary sources but it's such a complete collection of information, it's tricky not to reply on it.
  • History of Parliament - Link - This is one of the sources that I've used sporadically mainly to confirm discrepancies elsewhere.
  • UK Administrative Boundaries - Link - Used to work out counties for constituencies.
  • Scottish Boundary Commission - Link - Details changes in Scottish constituencies (some of which are quite complex!)
  • Office for National Statistics - Link - Also have constituency maps, not sure anything from here actually made it into the site though.
  • Open Data NI - Link More data, specific to Northern Ireland
